### Patch Kraft
- Definition：The brand name the project is being rebranded to (replacing the prior 'Patch Kraze' / 'PatchByte' naming). The conversation instructs to change the site name everywhere to Patch Kraft and apply a circular badge logo reading 'CUSTOM PATCHES & APPARELS'.
- Aliases：Patch Kraze、PatchByte

### PatchByte
- Definition：Internal project codename used in comments and scripts; also the npm package name. Refers to the custom frontend layer that intercepts Shopify-style fetch calls and routes cart/contact data to Supabase.
