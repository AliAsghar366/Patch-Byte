Product Details

Embroidered patches from Patch Kraft offer a vibrant and professional way to feature designs on apparel such as clothing, hats, jackets, or backpacks. Crafted from durable materials with precise embroidery, these custom patches ensure strong and long-lasting adherence to your chosen items.

You can personalize your patches extensively, giving you full control over their appearance. Choose from our three backing options: heat-applied (our most popular), peel-and-stick, and Velcro.

Want Embroidery, but your design may be too detailed? No worries, we will simply upgrade you to our Print + Stich method which will include embroidery and our sublimated print for the finer details.

FAQ

+ What Are Embroidered Patches?
Embroidered patches are decorative pieces of fabric featuring designs created by stitching colored threads onto a base material.
+ How Are Embroidered Patches Made?
Embroidered patches are made using computerized embroidery machines that stitch intricate designs onto a fabric backing.
+ What Are The Applications Of Embroidered Patches?
Used across many industries including military, law enforcement, corporate branding, sports teams, etc.
+ Why Choose Embroidered Patches?
Durability, professional appearance, and versatility. Withstand repeated washing and provide a classic textured look.
+ How To Attach Embroidered Patches
Iron-on, sew-on, velcro backing, or adhesive backing. Heat-press application is our most popular method.
+ What Types Of Embroidered Patches Do You Offer?
Standard embroidered, 3D puff, metallic thread, glow-in-the-dark, and more.
+ What Makes Embroidered Patches Different?
Textured, dimensional appearance created by actual thread stitching. Premium look and feel compared to printed.
